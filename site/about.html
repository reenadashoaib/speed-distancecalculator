About this calculator

Speed Distance Calculator answers one question quickly: if you know two of speed, distance and time, what is the third?

Students use it to check physics and maths homework. Runners and cyclists use it to plan race pace. Drivers, sailors and pilots use it to estimate journey times in the units they work in.

How results are worked out

Every value is converted to metres and seconds, the formula is applied, and the answer is converted back into the unit you chose. We use exact conversion factors: 1 mile is 1,609.344 metres and 1 nautical mile is 1,852 metres.

Results are averages over the distance you enter. Real journeys include stops, hills and traffic, so treat them as estimates for planning.
