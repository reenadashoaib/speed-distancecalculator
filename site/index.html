    The speed, distance and time formula

    Speed tells you how much distance is covered in a unit of time. Once you know any two of the three, the third follows from one of these:

    S = D ÷ T
    Speed is distance divided by time
    D = S × T
    Distance is speed multiplied by time
    T = D ÷ S
    Time is distance divided by speed

    The units have to agree. If speed is in kilometres per hour, distance must be in kilometres and time in hours. The calculator does those conversions for you, which is where most hand calculations go wrong: 1 hour 30 minutes is 1.5 hours, not 1.3.

    Worked examples

    • How fast was the drive?

      You drove 180 km and it took 2 hours 15 minutes.

      2 h 15 min = 2.25 h  →  180 ÷ 2.25 = 80 km/h

    • How far will I get?

      You cycle at 15 mph for 40 minutes.

      40 min = 0.667 h  →  15 × 0.667 = 10 miles

    • How long will the marathon take?

      You can hold 10.5 km/h for 42.195 km.

      42.195 ÷ 10.5 = 4.019 h  →  4 h 1 min 7 s

    Questions

    How do I calculate speed?

    Divide the distance by the time taken. 100 km in 2 hours is 100 ÷ 2 = 50 km/h. Convert minutes to hours first by dividing by 60.

    How do I convert km/h to mph?

    Multiply by 0.6214. To go from mph to km/h, multiply by 1.6093. 100 km/h is about 62 mph.

    How do I turn minutes into hours for the formula?

    Divide by 60. 45 minutes is 0.75 hours and 1 hour 20 minutes is 1.333 hours. You can type hours, minutes and seconds separately in the calculator and it will do this for you.

    What is the difference between speed and velocity?

    Speed is how fast something moves. Velocity is speed in a particular direction. For journeys, race times and fuel planning, speed is what you need.

    Is the answer my average speed?

    Yes. The result is the average over the whole distance, including any slow sections or stops that are part of the time you entered.

    What is a knot?

    One nautical mile per hour, used at sea and in aviation. A nautical mile is 1,852 metres, so 1 knot is 1.852 km/h or about 1.15 mph.
